Your key, your compute, no waiting

PromptGym runs on Groq's free tier, and that pool is shared by everyone on the site. It is small — a busy hour empties it. Bring your own free Groq key and you never queue behind anyone.

Get one at console.groq.com/keys — free, and it takes about a minute.

The key is kept in this browser's local storage and nowhere else. It is sent with each run over HTTPS, used for that run, and never written to our database or our logs. Removing it here removes it. Runs on your own key are graded by the same pinned judge, so they still count on the leaderboard.
