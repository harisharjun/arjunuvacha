      Terms of play

      Last updated 26 September 2026

      A recreational project

      PromptGym is a personal, non-commercial project by Arjun Uvacha, built for learning and for fun. It comes with no warranty: scores, rankings and the site itself may change, reset or disappear at any time.

      What is shown publicly

      If you sign in with Google, your Google display name and profile picture are shown on the public leaderboard, and next to any result you choose to share.

      Results are private unless you share them. A shared result shows your name, photo, score and the date it was run. Its prompt is readable only by players who have passed that challenge themselves. You can unshare at any time.

      What is stored

      • An account id, and — once you sign in — your Google display name and profile picture URL.
      • The prompts you run, their scores, and when you ran them.
      • Any feedback you send, with your Google name and email address and the page you sent it from, so Arjun can reply. It is emailed to him through Resend.

      Prompts are sent to be run and graded — to Groq for guests, and to OpenAI for signed-in players — and to Cloudflare to measure similarity. Don't put anything personal or confidential in a prompt.

      Fair use

      To keep the site running for everyone, guests can submit 5 prompts an hour, and signed-in players 3 a minute, 15 an hour and 100 a day. The site may pause runs for everyone if it reaches its daily limit.

      Your consent

      By playing — signed in or as a guest — you agree to these terms. If you don't, please don't use the site.

      Questions

      Signed in, use the Feedback button at the bottom of the page. Otherwise, reach Arjun through arjunuvacha.com.

      Leaderboard

      How ranking works
      1. Most challenges passed at the hardest level, then the next level down — one Hard beats any number of Easy.
      2. Tied? Most points at the hardest level.
      3. Still tied? Fewest prompt characters in total.
      4. Still tied? Fewest attempts.
      Only signed-in players are ranked.
