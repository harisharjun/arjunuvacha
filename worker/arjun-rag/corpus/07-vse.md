# Vishwa Samudra Engineering — Product Consultant (Mar 2023 – Jan 2025)

## Digital transformation of a construction conglomerate
Vishwa Samudra Engineering (VSE) is a construction company with $350M+ turnover and a 10,000+ employee conglomerate. Arjun started as a part-time consultant streamlining head-office processes and grew into leading product for the company's digital transformation, reporting to the MD and leading a team of 10 (2 Associate PMs, 4 Business Analysts, 2 Analysts, plus external developers). He implemented an org-wide ERP covering procurement, contracts, accounting, and finance — cutting approval delays from weeks to days, reducing weekly issues from 22 to 3, cutting resolution time from 3 days to 1, and enabling processing of ₹400+ crore of monthly purchase and work orders.

## Product strategy for the sites
Arjun identified that construction's biggest unaddressed problems live on project sites: material tracking, quality, safety, progress tracking. Rule of thumb: ~1% of construction cost leaks as unaccounted expenses (₹1 crore/month on ₹100 crore of bulk material) — his objective was to claw back that pie and make VSE technology-first. His four-part strategy: bridge the site-to-head-office gap (product, process, people); build in-house for material/quality/safety while integrating external tools for progress tracking; build an integrated data platform for insights, monitoring, and real-time interventions; keep ERP for accounting/admin only. He implemented OKRs for his team and built PowerBI dashboards and daily reports for real-time monitoring, plus an industry-first end-to-end intervention system letting head office act on site issues in real time.

## Products built: TrackZ, KwartZ, Safety Eye, BeatZ, WingZ, CCC
The full product suite Arjun's team built at Vishwa Samudra: TrackZ (material management) — weighbridge-verified tracking of bulk material deliveries; 40% of all received material tracked across projects, attacking pilferage, the single biggest cost leak. KwartZ (quality management) — 100+ lab test types and site inspection types digitised; 15,000+ lab tests and 4,000+ site inspections done and reviewed; lab technicians' result-calculation time cut from 30 minutes to 3 minutes; real-time quality alerts to the chief quality controller. Safety Eye (safety management) — QR-code issue reporting by on-site personnel plus safety trainings, checklists, and drills; 2,100+ safety issues resolved, resolution time down from up to 25 days to 5 days. BeatZ (security management) — patrolling duties, checklist activities, material movement tracking, incident and SOS reporting across sites. WingZ (shutter management) — tracking utility and repeatability of shuttering material so construction progress never stalls. Command Control Center (CCC) — the nerve center integrating real-time data from all applications for monitoring, insights, and head-office interventions. Overall: site safety incidents fell from 25+ to under 10 per quarter, fatalities to zero, and $11.5M in quarterly operations were digitised end-to-end for non-technical field users.

## Working without data
Arjun cites VSE as his "decision without data" environment: a domain he knew nothing about (construction), no baseline metrics, and users who had never used software. He built the product strategy from qualitative insight, competitor observation, ethics/safety reasoning, and long-term thinking — designing workflows for non-technical field users from first principles, then instrumenting everything so future decisions could be data-driven. The challenges he names: formulating experiments in a non-digital culture, site-team adoption, and managing external developers with poor estimation habits.
