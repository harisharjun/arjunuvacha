# Edureka — Associate PM, Growth & Learning (Nov 2019 – Jan 2021)

## Role overview
At Edureka (consumer ed-tech, Bengaluru), Arjun was Associate PM for Growth & Learning on the post-graduate programs (PGP) vertical. Headline results: built a consumer-facing enrollment product 0→1 and scaled revenue 10× (₹500K → ₹5M monthly, i.e., ₹5L → ₹50L) in 6 months; improved traffic-to-lead conversion from 3.6% to 4.8%; improved lead-to-payment conversion from 0.9% to 1.4%; reduced 'hot-lead to sale' TAT by 33% from 30 to 20 days.

## Full-stack program: 0→1 in 3 days with no engineers
Edureka served working professionals, but ~1.5M fresh engineering graduates a year lacked industry skills — a big untapped segment. Arjun was asked to build an MVP for a full-stack web development program in 10 business days with almost no engineering support. He studied the new segment (not earning, low propensity to pay, tech-savvy, time-rich, community-active), then assembled the whole acquisition product from no-code tools: an Unbounce landing page he built himself, GoToWebinar demos, Zapier automations wiring registrations and attendance into Google Sheets, and a Razorpay payment page. A 3-step funnel (register → attend demo → pay) filtered genuine candidates. The MVP was live in 3 days — a week ahead of deadline. First month: 47–48 enrollments at ₹10K each (~₹4.8L), CPL ₹40, CAC ~₹6,000.

## Full-stack program: scaling 10× through funnel iteration
Arjun broke the funnel into stages (registered → attended → interested → enrolled), interviewed ~50 students across stages, and analysed conversion data. Leads grew from 6,000 (month 1) to 18,000 (month 6). Traffic-to-demo-registration rose 8% → 13% (mobile-friendly asset-light page, first-fold CTAs, "job-ready" copy A/B tests, participation certificates, multiple slots). Demo attendance rose 30% → 42% (HubSpot reminder emails/SMS at T-1 day and T-30 min). Demo-to-payment rose 2% → 3.1% (automated lead scoring on work experience, education, demo engagement, poll answers; drip campaigns; ₹1,000 advance-payment page; EMI/BNPL options). Traffic-to-enrollment conversion rose 0.05% → 0.17%; month 6 revenue was ~₹43–50L vs ~₹4.7L in month 1 (~10×). The workflow was then productionised: dedicated landing page, Zoho CRM journeys, marketing campaigns, per-user payment links.

## PGP landing page revamp — research-driven
When the AI/ML PG program page underperformed (low conversion vs market, repeat customer questions, slow page speed), Arjun ran qualitative research (15 customer interviews, 300+ sales call recordings across ~70 customers, 7 sales/ops interviews) plus quantitative work (Google Analytics, PageSpeed). Findings: ~90% bought because of the NITW/IITG university collaboration (which the page under-played); buyers were upskilling for promotions/transitions and always comparison-shopped; the page did feature-selling when customers needed value-selling. He rebuilt the page around credibility (university-verified curriculum, testimonials, instructors, placement/career assistance, live training, lifetime access, 24×7 support) and A/B tested the new variant 50/50 for 30 days — conversion improved decisively (traffic-to-lead 3.6% → 4.8%) and the new page shipped for good.

## Learning-experience research
As Learning Experience PM, Arjun interviewed 12 AI/ML program completers to build personas and 28 learners on practice/assessment challenges — finding learners fumbled syntax, forgot concepts, and lacked interview confidence due to weak feedback loops. For full-stack, he interviewed 11 learners plus competitor learners, industry developers, and hiring teams — finding learners struggled to decompose problems and got late assignment feedback. These insights drove program and product changes.
