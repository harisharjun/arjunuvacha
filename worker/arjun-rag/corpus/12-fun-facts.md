## Small human details
The site's name, Arjun Uvacha, is Sanskrit for "Arjun said" — in the Bhagavad Gita it marks the questioner's turn to speak, and Arjun sees himself as the perpetual questioner. He once cut an internal ops process from 66 seconds to 28 and jokes that he notices when a coffee order takes too long. During his Darwinbox listening tour he visited 11 cities — ask him which one had the best coffee.
