# The builder side — AI Lab and personal projects

## AI Lab: what Arjun builds outside work
Arjun designs, vibe-codes, and ships AI products end-to-end on nights and weekends. Current projects: Anvi Chatbot — a conversational AI assistant he built end-to-end (prompt design, context handling, UX), live at https://testchat-169af.web.app/; Career Kria (careerkria.com, formerly launched as Career Mantra) — an AI-powered career practice and assessment SaaS built fully solo, no co-founder or dedicated engineer: Next.js/React and FastAPI/Python on PostgreSQL, using the OpenAI API (structured output schemas, function calling, AI-scored assessments), Deepgram for voice, and Razorpay for payments, built end-to-end in Claude and Cursor; Tax Regime Copilot (arjunuvacha.com/tax-copilot) — a chat-driven old-vs-new Indian income tax regime calculator for salaried people: reads a payslip, offer letter, or salary statement entirely in the browser (nothing uploaded), runs every rupee through a deterministic rules engine so the AI never does the arithmetic, and only uses a model (via a Cloudflare Worker backend, key never exposed client-side) to explain the numbers and apply plain-language edits like "add ELSS of 50,000"; and a gift project — an app he built for his wife's birthday to help relieve her stress. It has two games to play, weekly letters he writes to her, and a conversation with "Krishna", where every reply is grounded in actual Bhagavad Gita verses through retrieval — no hallucinated scripture, only real verses interpreted with warmth. The "Ask Arjun" chatbot on arjunuvacha.com is also his build — the assistant answering questions about him is itself proof of his AI-building skills.

## Why the lab matters
Arjun's philosophy: a PM who builds AI products by hand keeps honest instincts — every prompt, edge case, and user complaint is his to fix. At work this shows up as the vibe-coded Employee Relations prototype that carried a greenlight meeting, and the POC where PM-authored PRDs directly generated working code. He calls AI tools the biggest change in what a PM can bring to a room: not a deck describing a product, but the product itself.
