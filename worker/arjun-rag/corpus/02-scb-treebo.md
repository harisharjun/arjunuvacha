# Standard Chartered and Treebo (2013–2019)

## Standard Chartered Bank (Sep 2013 – Mar 2015)
Arjun's first job after his MBA was Senior Financial Analyst at Standard Chartered (Scope International). He worked in the Change Management team, ensuring that technological and infrastructural changes by the bank did not impact service delivery and business from a performance and risk point of view. This grounding in risk-aware change management later informed his careful approach to shipping changes in large enterprise products.

## Treebo Hotels — role and results (Mar 2017 – Jul 2019)
At Treebo Hotels (hospitality tech), Arjun was Regional Sales Lead for Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, progressing from Territory Sales Manager (50+ clients captured in Hitech City in 9 months) to Territory Sales Lead managing a team of 9–10 across Hyderabad, Vizag, and Vijayawada. Headline results: grew monthly revenue from ₹1.8M to ₹4.7M (2.5×) in about 6 months; onboarded 150+ corporate clients over 2.5 years; reduced Days Sales Outstanding from over 100 days to 55 days in 8 months; won Treebo's elite 'Millenia' Award three times for consistently achieving 1,000+ room nights a month.

## Treebo — regional turnaround playbook
Arjun transformed a low-performing region into one of Treebo's best. His playbook: market survey, lead identification, breaking the region into pockets, a month of intensive recce ("hunting") per pocket, then consistent follow-up ("farming"). He managed a portfolio of 1,500+ enterprise accounts, classifying leads into thematic buckets by potential, bottlenecks, and historic issues. Example: many leads were stalled on the lack of a Bill-to-Company (BTC) credit option — he built a business case quantifying the missed revenue and won an exception approval with tighter terms (next BTC extension only if the previous payment cleared within 15 days). He set individual targets and incentives and ran weekly tracking with course corrections.

## Treebo — earning trust under crisis (Tech Mahindra story)
Tech Mahindra, one of the region's biggest clients, booked hotel stays for large rotating batches of freshers over 30–45 days. When a power outage knocked out a chunk of rooms at one hotel and the Admin Manager expressed strong displeasure, Arjun moved fast: learned the outage would last 3+ days, got regional-head approval to arrange transportation, moved occupants to better rooms in another hotel the same evening, stationed a sales manager on-premise to oversee logistics, and personally visited the Admin Manager the next day to apologise. The swift recovery reinstated the client's trust and the business continued without disruption. Arjun tells this as his "earning trust" story: trust is rebuilt through speed and personal ownership, not explanations.
