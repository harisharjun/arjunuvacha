# Education and early achievements

## Academics
Arjun completed his B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from NIT Warangal (2007–2011, CGPA 7.95) as a merit scholar, and an MBA in General Management from Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Learning (SSSIHL), Puttaparthi (2011–2013, CGPA 7.32), where he topped Semester II. MBA electives included Entrepreneurship and Innovation, Corporate Financial Strategy, Mergers and Acquisitions, and Marketing Strategy. School: Class XII with 97% (100% in Mathematics), Class X with 87% (97% in Mathematics).

## Entrance exam ranks and honors
Arjun's competitive exam record: AIEEE All India Rank 488, IIT-JEE All India Rank 3,135, EAMCET All India Rank 57, and a top-50 finish in the State Level Mathematics Olympiad. He held the Bharti Merit-cum-Means Scholarship (funded by the Bharti Foundation) for all four consecutive years at NIT Warangal. ## Early leadership
At NIT Warangal, Arjun was Logistics Head for Technozion (2010), the institute's technical fest — he led a sub-core team of 8 plus 30+ volunteers to organize logistics for 5,000+ students. At SSSIHL he was Creative Head for Sai Publications, leading a 4-member team designing book covers (15,000+ copies printed), 7 e-books, greeting cards (30,000+ printed), a blog, and a website.

## Early technical projects
In 2010 Arjun did a 2-month research internship at IIT Bombay on Optimization Techniques for Cognitive Radio, and a game theory project on cognitive radio at NIT Warangal. In 2012 he ran a Total Quality Management project analysing the 4Ps for the Sai Blossom product category of Radio Sai. In 2016 he independently built a web portal (Bootstrap, HTML, CSS) for the Harvard South Asia Institute's Women's Empowerment Program, which brought together 50 organizations from 13 states in India.
