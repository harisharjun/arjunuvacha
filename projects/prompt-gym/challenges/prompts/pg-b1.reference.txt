Extract the invoice into JSON: {"line_items":[{"description":"...","amount":n}],
"stated_total":n,"computed_total":n,"total_matches":true|false}.
Amounts are numbers, not strings — strip commas and currency symbols, keep
the sign (discounts and credits are negative). Copy stated_total exactly as
printed on the invoice, even if it looks wrong. Compute computed_total by
adding the line items. Set total_matches to whether the two are equal.
Never adjust a line item or the stated total to make them agree.
Output only the JSON.
