How this works, and what it deliberately won't do

Every rupee on the result page is computed by a deterministic engine in this file, from a rulepack of current-year rates. No language model touches the arithmetic — a model is used only to answer follow-up questions in plain English, through a shared backend with no setup needed.

The engine is checked against 21 hand-built salary profiles whose expected answers were computed by a second, independently written implementation — including the ₹12 lakh rebate cliff, marginal relief just past it, the ₹50 lakh surcharge boundary, and a profile that sits ten rupees from the break-even point.

Out of scope on purpose: business or freelance income, capital gains, foreign assets, non-resident status, more than two employers. You'll be asked about these up front and told plainly if you need a CA.
